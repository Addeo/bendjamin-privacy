Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Franklin’s diary (Bendgamine) is developed by Sergei Kosilov. This policy describes version 1.1.1 of the iOS app.

Your journal stays on your device

No account is required. Projects, virtue marks, plans, journal entries and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not upload that information to the developer or a cloud account. Insights and coaching prompts are generated locally, without sending your journal to an external AI provider.

The app does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not ask for access to your contacts or location. Optional reminders use local notifications after you grant notification permission; you can disable them in the app or iOS Settings.

Backups and deletion

You may export or import a local JSON backup in Settings. A backup may contain private journal information: keep it somewhere safe and share it only with people you trust. The developer does not receive your exported file unless you separately choose to send it.

To erase the diary from this device, use the erase-data action in Settings and confirm the warnings. Exported copies and device backups are separate; delete those separately if you no longer want them. The app does not provide developer-operated cloud recovery.

Optional external links and gifts

Settings includes an optional link to the developer’s Destream gift page. It opens your system browser. Gifts are voluntary, do not unlock content or services and do not affect access to any app feature. The app does not process payment details or receive payment-status data. Your journal is not included in the link.

When you visit Destream, its own privacy policy and terms govern information you submit there. Similarly, your browser connects to the public website host, GitHub Pages, when you open privacy or support pages. These services may receive ordinary web-request information such as your IP address. No journal data is added by the app to these URLs. This website adds no analytics or tracking scripts.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, and uses it to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or your journal backup unless it is genuinely necessary and you intend to share it. Support email is not required to use the app.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Future cloud or payment features, if introduced, require an updated explanation before use.
