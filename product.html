A thoughtful daily practice · By Sergei Kosilov

13 virtues.
One weekly focus.

A free personal journal inspired by Benjamin Franklin’s method. Choose a weekly virtue, record your daily practice and make time for reflection.

For iPhone & iPad · App Store release in preparation

A small ritual, every day

Write an intention in the morning. Mark what went well — or what you want to improve. Return in the evening for a quiet moment of reflection.

Your week, with intention

  • Track all 13 virtues with practice marks or black spots.
  • Write morning intentions and evening reflections.
  • Make a weekly plan and review local insights.
  • Set optional local reminders.

A journal that feels like yours

  • Choose light or dark appearance and customize virtue icons.
  • Use one of eight interface languages.
  • Export and import your own backups.
  • Keep your journal on your device — no account required.

Free, without feature locks

No account, subscription or paid feature lock. Optional personal gifts do not unlock content or services. Your daily practice is yours, whether you give or not.
