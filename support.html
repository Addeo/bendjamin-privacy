Getting started

Complete the introduction, choose a focus virtue and select practice marks or black-spot mode. You do not need to create an account.

Daily practice

Mark virtues in the tracker, write a morning intention or evening reflection, and review your weekly plan. Local insights help you reflect on your progress.

Back up or move your journal

Use Settings to export a JSON copy before changing devices. Import that file on the other device. There is no automatic cloud sync. Keep your backup safe: it may contain private journal entries.

Reminders

Enable reminders in the app and allow notifications in iOS Settings. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Erase your data

Use the erase-data action in Settings and confirm the warnings. This is permanent; save a backup first if needed. Delete separate exported files or device backups yourself.

Price and voluntary gifts

All features are free, with no subscription or feature lock. An optional personal gift adds no feature, content or reward. The app does not process payment details or receive payment-status data.

Privacy comes first

Your journal stays on your device. Please read the Privacy Policy before sharing information in a support request.
